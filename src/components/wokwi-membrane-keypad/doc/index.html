Membrane Keypad (4x4)

A standard 4×4 matrix keypad with 16 keys (0–9, A–D, *, #). Connect Row pins (R1–R4) to Arduino digital output pins, and Column pins (C1–C4) to Arduino digital input pins configured as INPUT_PULLUP.

Usage

In your sketch, drive rows LOW one at a time and read which column goes LOW to detect the pressed key. A popular library is Keypad.h by Mark Stanley & Alexander Brevig.
