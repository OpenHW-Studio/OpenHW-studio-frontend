Clock Generator

Logic

An adjustable digital clock generator. It outputs a continuous square wave oscillating between HIGH (5V) and LOW (0V). You can adjust the frequency and units (Hz, KHz, MHz) using its property panel.

Attributes (Properties Panel)

AttributeTypeDescription
FrequencyNumberThe base number for frequency (e.g. "10").
UnitsSelectThe multiplier: Hz, KHz, or MHz. (e.g., 10 + KHz = 10,000 cycles/sec).

Pins

PinTypeDescription
OUTDigital OutputProduces the continuously toggling square wave.

Wiring


Clock OUT ────► CLK input on D Flip-Flop
          ────► Counter devices
          ────► Arduino D2 (for interrupt counting)
Tip: Right-click the Clock Generator to open the Properties Panel where you can set your desired Frequency and Units. In a browser environment, extreme frequencies (e.g., MHz) might be capped smoothly to match your browser's frame rate capabilities visually, but logical propagation steps will still simulate correctly.
