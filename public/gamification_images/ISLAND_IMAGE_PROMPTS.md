# Island Image Generation Prompts

> All images: **1:1 square ratio**, recommended render size **1024×1024** or **1500×1500**  
> Format: **PNG with white background** (same as island_led.png and island_rgb.png)  
> Drop finished files into: `/public/gamification_images/`  
> ✅ Already done: `island_led.png`, `island_rgb.png`, `adventure_map_bg.png`

## Style Reference (include this base in EVERY prompt)

```
3D isometric low-poly cartoon floating island, viewed from a slight elevated 3/4 angle.
The island has a flat hexagonal grass top surface with a vibrant low-poly green gradient,
surrounded by chunky low-poly rocky brown-tan cliff sides that drop straight down with
large angular stone blocks. Two or three cartoonish low-poly palm trees on the left and
right edges of the island top. The electronic component sits in the CENTER of the island
on a small flat square metallic bolted platform/pedestal. White background. Soft warm
directional lighting from top-left. Subtle color glow from the component onto the grass
surface. Centered in frame with slight bottom margin. No text, no shadows on background.
Game asset style, same art as Duolingo world map islands.
```
